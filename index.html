New Era University K–12 Program

Building Character, Knowledge, and Leadership for the Future.

The K–12 Program of New Era University is committed to providing quality education that develops students academically, morally, and socially. The program prepares learners with the knowledge and skills necessary for higher education and future careers.

About Our Program

Our Mission

Provide quality education anchored on Christian values with the prime purpose of bringing honor and glory to God.

Our Vision

A world-class Institution of learning with a unique Christian culture of excellence, discipline, and service to humanity.

Educational Philosophy

Godliness is the foundation of knowledge.

Commitment to Student Development

Our program is dedicated to the holistic growth of every student, focusing on academic excellence, character formation, leadership skills, and community engagement to prepare them for success in higher education and beyond.

Academic Levels

Kindergarten

  • Early childhood development focus
  • Basic literacy and numeracy skills
  • Social and emotional learning
  • Play-based learning approach

Elementary School

  • Strong foundation in core academic subjects
  • Character development and values education
  • Engaging and interactive learning activities
  • Development of critical thinking skills

Junior High School

  • In-depth study of core academic subjects
  • Skill development for problem-solving
  • Exploration of various interests and talents
  • Preparation for advanced studies

Senior High School

  • Specialized academic tracks (STEM, HUMSS, ABM, GAS)
  • Intensive preparation for college and careers
  • Leadership and community service opportunities
  • Research and innovation projects

Programs & Curriculum

Academic Subjects

  • Mathematics (Algebra, Geometry, Calculus)
  • Sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Earth Science)
  • English Language and Literature
  • Filipino Language and Literature
  • Social Studies (History, Geography, Economics)
  • Arts and Music Education
  • Physical Education and Health

Specialized Programs

  • STEM Education: Fostering innovation in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics.
  • Humanities & Social Sciences: Developing critical thinking and understanding of human society.
  • Leadership & Values Education: Nurturing ethical leaders with strong moral foundations.
  • Co-curricular Programs: Enhancing learning through clubs, competitions, and special projects.
  • Digital Literacy: Equipping students with essential 21st-century technology skills.

Student Opportunities

Academic Competitions
Students are encouraged to participate in various academic competitions, including Math Olympiads, Science Fairs, Debate Tournaments, and Essay Writing Contests, to challenge themselves and showcase their talents.
Student Leadership Programs
Our leadership programs empower students to develop essential leadership skills through student councils, peer mentoring, and various school-wide initiatives, fostering responsibility and initiative.
Clubs and Organizations
A wide array of clubs and organizations caters to diverse interests, including robotics, creative writing, environmental advocacy, performing arts, and sports, promoting teamwork and personal growth.
Community Involvement
Students actively participate in community service projects, outreach programs, and environmental initiatives, instilling a sense of social responsibility and civic engagement.

Admissions

Admission Requirements

  • Completed Application Form
  • Original Report Card (Form 138)
  • Good Moral Character Certificate
  • Birth Certificate (PSA Authenticated)
  • Two (2) 2x2 colored ID pictures
  • Entrance Examination Fee

Application Procedures

  1. Submit complete requirements to the Admissions Office.
  2. Pay the Entrance Examination Fee at the Cashier.
  3. Take the Entrance Examination on the scheduled date.
  4. Attend the interview with the Admissions Committee.
  5. Receive the Admission Slip upon successful evaluation.
